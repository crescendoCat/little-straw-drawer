Privacy Policy — Mi Mi Draw Machine

Last updated: 2026-09-19

Mi Mi Draw Machine ("the App") is a free web app for drawing straws. This page explains what data the App handles and how.

1. Data on your device

Your straws, presets, drawing history and settings are stored only in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device unless you back them up to Google Drive (section 2). Clearing site data removes them.

2. Google Drive backup (optional)

When you click "Link Google Drive", the App requests the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. It grants access only to a hidden, app-specific folder in your own Google Drive; the App cannot see or change any other file.

  • What is stored: one file, little-straw-drawer.json, containing your straws, presets, drawing history and settings.
  • When: only when you press "Save to Drive", "Restore from Drive" or choose an option in the first-link dialog. There is no automatic sync.
  • How: your browser talks to Google directly. The App has no server; the developer never receives, stores or sees your data or Google account details.
  • Access token: kept in browser memory only and expires after about one hour.
  • Unlinking: "Unlink Google Drive" revokes the token and forgets the link on this device. The backup stays in your Drive until you delete it under Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps, or revoke access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never sold, used for advertising, or read by humans.

3. Analytics

The site loads Google Analytics and Cloudflare Web Analytics, which collect aggregated technical data (page views, approximate region, browser, device). Cloudflare Web Analytics uses no cookies; Google Analytics may, and can be blocked in your browser. Analytics data is never linked to your straws or Drive data.

4. No accounts or personal data collected by the developer

There is no sign-up, and the App does not ask for your name, e-mail or other personal information.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

6. Changes

Updates are reflected in the "Last updated" date; material changes are announced in the App's "What's new" dialog.

隱私權政策 — Mi Mi Draw Machine

最後更新:2026-09-19。本中文版僅供參考,如有歧義以英文版為準。

Mi Mi Draw Machine(以下稱「本 App」)是一個免費的網頁抽籤工具。本頁說明本 App 會處理哪些資料,以及處理方式。

1. 儲存在你裝置上的資料

你的籤、預設集、抽籤紀錄與設定只儲存在瀏覽器的 local storage。除非你選擇備份到 Google Drive(第 2 節),資料不會離開你的裝置。清除網站資料即會刪除它們。

2. Google Drive 備份(選用)

當你點擊「Link Google Drive」,本 App 會請求 https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata 權限。此權限只能存取你自己 Google Drive 中一個隱藏的、專屬於本 App 的資料夾;本 App 無法看見或修改任何其他檔案。

  • 儲存內容:單一檔案 little-straw-drawer.json,內含你的籤、預設集、抽籤紀錄與設定。
  • 儲存時機:只在你按下「Save to Drive」「Restore from Drive」或在首次連結對話框中做出選擇時。沒有自動同步。
  • 方式:你的瀏覽器直接與 Google 溝通。本 App 沒有伺服器;開發者不會接收、儲存或看到你的資料或 Google 帳號資訊。
  • 存取權杖:只保存在瀏覽器記憶體中,約一小時後失效。
  • 解除連結:「Unlink Google Drive」會撤銷權杖並忘記此裝置的連結。備份檔仍留在你的 Drive 中,直到你在 Google Drive → 設定 → 管理應用程式 刪除,或在 https://myaccount.google.com/permissions 撤銷存取權。

本 App 對自 Google API 取得之資訊的使用,遵守 Google API 服務使用者資料政策,包含其「有限使用」規範。Google 使用者資料絕不會被出售、用於廣告或由人工閱讀。

3. 流量分析

本網站載入 Google Analytics 與 Cloudflare Web Analytics,收集彙整後的技術性資料(頁面瀏覽數、大致地區、瀏覽器、裝置)。Cloudflare Web Analytics 不使用 cookie;Google Analytics 可能使用,且可在瀏覽器中封鎖。分析資料絕不會與你的籤或 Drive 資料連結。

4. 開發者不收集帳號或個人資料

本 App 沒有註冊機制,也不會詢問你的姓名、電子郵件或其他個人資訊。

5. 兒童

本 App 並非針對 13 歲以下兒童設計,亦不會在知情的情況下收集其資料。

6. 政策變更

更新會反映在「最後更新」日期;重大變更會在本 App 的「What's new」對話框中公告。
